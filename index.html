Why Classical Hatha Yoga?

"
We want to bring back classical yoga in its purest form—not studio yoga, not book yoga, not the kind of things that are being done in the world—but proper classical yoga, which is a phenomenally powerful science.
— Sadhguru

The Incredible Power of Classical Hatha Yoga

  • 1
    Restoring Pure Classical Yoga: Our vision is to bring back Classical Hatha Yoga in its purest form—a phenomenally powerful science, distinctly different from the diluted studio or book yoga common in the world today.
  • 2
    An Unaltered Ancient Lineage: This is a profound tradition that has survived for millennia. The teachers of this lineage have carefully preserved the original practices with immense reverence, without altering them or leaving their own "signatures" behind.
  • 3
    Breathing Life into the Mechanics: Classical yoga goes far beyond physical mechanics or seeking superficial physical benefits. True yoga requires investing your life into the profoundness of the practice, breathing life into the postures to make them a living process.
  • 4
    Transmission Through Energy: Authentic yoga cannot be taught merely through verbal instruction. It must be transmitted by creating a powerful vessel of energy, allowing the practice to truly come alive within you.
  • 5
    Offering the Genuine Science: At a time when serious distortions of yoga are rampant across the globe, providing the authentic, uncorrupted science of Classical Hatha Yoga is a tremendous responsibility.
  • 6
    Setting the Ultimate Standard: By living and offering this ancient practice in a deeply inspiring and genuine way, Classical Hatha Yoga sets the ultimate standard for true wellbeing and human transformation.

You’ve Been Doing Yoga Wrong – Here’s Why

"
Yoga is not about how beautifully one can bend, but how deeply one can be still.
— Sadhguru

5 Things You Didn't Know About Classical Yoga

  • 1
    No Physical Adjustments: Teachers do not physically touch or adjust you. Touch disturbs your unique energy geometry; alignment must come from your own internal awareness.
  • 2
    No Music or Mirrors: Silence is sacred. Music draws your attention outward, and mirrors turn yoga into a performance. Keeping your eyes closed lets you stop looking at yourself and start being with yourself.
  • 3
    Empty Stomach is Crucial: Practicing on a full stomach forces the body to focus on digestion. An empty stomach allows your energies to rise upward, making the body light and vibrant.
  • 4
    No Hot Yoga or Stimulants: Yoga is an inner process, not a sweaty workout. Practicing in excessive heat causes cellular damage, and taking stimulants like alcohol completely disrupts your inner equilibrium.
  • 5
    Flexibility is Not the Goal: Bending more doesn't mean you are progressing. Physical flexibility is merely a preparation. True yoga begins when your body and mind become entirely still.

Our Practices

Upa-Yoga

Upa-yoga is a simple yet powerful system of exercise that activate the joints, muscles and energy system. Based on a sophisticated understanding of the body’s mechanics, Upa-yoga brings great ease to the whole system. It relieves physical stress and tiredness. Within the human system, the energy flows along 72,000 pathways called nadis. At the joints, the nadis form energy nodes, making the joints into storehouses of energy. Upa-yoga activates this energy and also lubricates the joints, creating an instant sense of alertness and liveliness. It rejuvenates the body after a period of inactivity and negates the effects of jetlag and long travel.

Angamardana

Angamardana is a system rooted in yoga that offers to everyone the opportunity to invigorate the body and to reach peak physical and mental health. “Angamardana” means gaining complete mastery over the limbs, organs and other parts of the body. True to its name, this practice revitalizes the body on all levels including the muscles, circulatory system, skeletal structure, nervous system, and the basic energy system. It strengthens the spine, builds physical strength, fitness and tenacity, taking years off the body. Designed by Sadhguru, Isha Angamardana needs no fitness equipment. It involves only the body and floor exercises that can be practiced anywhere, even during travel.

Surya Shakti

"Surya" means "Sun," and "Shakti" means "energy". It is a dynamic form of the Sun Salutation designed to build the physical body and strengthen sinews. In Yoga, we give importance to the sinews that hold the skeletal system and the whole body together. When we do any yogic practice, which is physical in nature, the focus is mainly to strengthen those, not to pump up your muscles. Strengthening the sinews of the body is what will endure for a long time and keep you well. Surya Shakti does this in a tremendous way.

Surya Kriya

Surya Kriya is a potent yogic practice of tremendous antiquity, designed as a holistic process for health, wellness, and complete inner well-being. “Surya” means “sun,” and “kriya” means “inner energy process.” Surya Kriya activates the solar plexus to raise the samat prana, or solar heat, in the system. It also balances a person’s left and right energy channels, leading to stability of the body and stillness of the mind. This strong foundation becomes the basis to explore higher dimensions of life.
Traditionally available only to select groups of yogis, Surya Kriya is being offered by Sadhguru as a comprehensive spiritual practice that is ideal for the hectic pace of today’s world.

Yogasanas

The word asana literally means a posture. Out of the innumerable asanas a body can assume, 84 have been identified as Yogasanas, through which one can transform the body and mind into a possibility for ultimate well-being. By consciously forming the body into a certain posture, one creates a conducive passage for energy flow that can elevate one’s consciousness.
Yogasanas are a way of aligning the inner system and adjusting it to the celestial geometry, becoming in sync with the existence, thus naturally achieving a state of health, joy, bliss and above all balance.

About the Teacher

Hi, my name is Sankalp. Since childhood, I naturally possessed a deep longing to experience higher dimensions of energy. In my early twenties, I tried various tools to explore this—learning yoga through books and videos—but the moment I stumbled upon the practices offered by Isha, I knew there was no looking back or searching elsewhere.

In March 2018, while in my 3rd year at Trinity College Dublin, I attended the Inner Engineering program in Dublin and had a profoundly transformative experience. This inspired me to learn furthur Isha practices and I learnt Classical Hatha Yoga from a local teacher.

I completed the rigorous Teacher Training Program at the Isha Yoga Center in India and became a Classical Hatha Yoga teacher, officially certified by Sadhguru Gurukulam, in December 2025.

After six years of dedicated daily practice, I am deeply moved to contribute to Sadhguru’s vision of global well-being by offering these transformative tools to the world.
